COAA-2025: Compact Object Astrophysics and Astronomy

December 10–12, 2025 · Organizer · IIT Jodhpur

I was part of the organizing team for the 1st International Conference on Compact Object Astrophysics and Astronomy (COAA-2025). This conference brought together leading researchers and students to discuss contemporary issues in the physics of ultra-dense matter found inside astrophysical compact objects. Our scientific sessions focused heavily on equations of state, neutron star cooling, gravitational waves, and pulsar phenomena. Alongside the intense academic discussions, we also had the pleasure of sharing the rich cultural heritage of Jodhpur with our colleagues.
