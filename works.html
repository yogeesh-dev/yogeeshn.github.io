Published research on the mechanisms behind neutron star spin-ups.

Modeling Large Glitches with Core Superfluidity in a Hybrid Star

DOI: 10.1103/2p7c-mj4x

This work investigates sudden spin-up events in pulsars. It explores how core superfluidity contributes to vortex pinning and glitch amplitudes, examining the potential role of quark pasta structures on the glitch mechanism.
